A company that talks about honesty law should publish its own ledger. Here is
where everything stands, in the only tense we allow ourselves.

**Live today**

- **Prism** — the design system, with full docs, a live component playground,
  an MCP server over its knowledge, and the five-pack token system every
  other site consumes. Live at [prism.nanisoft.com](https://prism.nanisoft.com).
- **Atlas** — the Digital Twin Platform's site: landing, nineteen pages of
  docs, blog, and the in-browser digital-twin playground that teaches its
  architecture (fully mocked, and labelled that way). Live at
  [atlas.nanisoft.com](https://atlas.nanisoft.com).
- **AlphaLens** — quantitative trading research for the Indian market: a live
  minute-by-minute market-data collector and its unified data contract are
  real and running; the research platform's site carries the honest tiers.
  Live at [alphalens.nanisoft.com](https://alphalens.nanisoft.com).
- **www** — this site: the company root, landing, about, and blog.

**In development**

- **Nexus** — the Agent Factory. Its architecture is designed and documented:
  orchestration on the Microsoft Agent Framework, per-issue worker containers,
  a Kanban feedback loop with a three-round ceiling. The sites you are reading
  were built by directed agents; the factory that industrializes that loop is
  being built now. Its site describes the design, never a shipped feature:
  [nexus.nanisoft.com](https://nexus.nanisoft.com).

**Designed, not built**

- Atlas's live data bindings and blast-radius simulation; AlphaLens's
  TradingAgents wiring and k3s deployment; the factory's multi-project
  scheduler at scale. Each is written up on its product's site as design, not
  as claim.

The ledger moves, and this post will age. That is what it is for — the next
one gets to correct it.
