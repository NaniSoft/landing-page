With this site, the nanisoft.com family is complete: five sites, one design
language.

- [prism.nanisoft.com](https://prism.nanisoft.com): the design system itself
- [nexus.nanisoft.com](https://nexus.nanisoft.com): the Agent Factory
- [atlas.nanisoft.com](https://atlas.nanisoft.com): the Digital Twin Platform
- [alphalens.nanisoft.com](https://alphalens.nanisoft.com): market research, quantified
- [www.nanisoft.com](https://www.nanisoft.com): the company root (you are here)

Every site wears [Prism](https://prism.nanisoft.com)'s Spectral Refraction
(Archivo Variable and JetBrains Mono, hairline structure, pastel packs in light
and beam-dark), and each product owns one pack: Nexus lavender, Atlas mint,
AlphaLens blush. Prism is not a fifth member of that set. It is the language the
set is written in, so its own site wears no pack at all, and a mark with no pack
is drawn as the spectrum rather than as a colourless dot. The company root is the
apex, so it paints itself in sky and lays the others on marks: pack colour marks
the products and the architecture, peach is left for what the factory builds next,
and the ground never changes.

That is also why this site shows all five marks on its landing rather than four.
The other four are products with a colour each; this one is the place they are
listed, so it wears the ground; and Prism is the one member with no hue of its
own, which the design system draws as a sweep of the whole set rather than as
nothing. A reader who counts the pastels on that page finds four, and the fifth
pastel is still unspent.

The parts every site shares are not copied. The header with its product
switcher, the footer with its registry-driven product grid, the flash-free
theme system. They ship from npm as [`@nanisoft/prism-ui`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@nanisoft/prism-ui),
and the sites consume them verbatim. When the chrome improves, five sites get
the improvement on the next version bump, and drift is impossible by
construction.

That is the point of a design system: not consistency as an aspiration, but
consistency as a fact about how the code is built.
